Why it matters

Two decisions. Countless lives changed.

Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from a willing donor. A single organ donor can save up to eight lives and improve dozens more through tissue donation. Lifeline exists to make both decisions easy to act on.

How it works

From registration to real impact.

1. Register

Create a donor profile with your blood group and, optionally, organ donor preferences.

2. Get Matched

Receive alerts when a nearby request matches your profile, or browse requests directly.

3. Donate

Visit a partner hospital or blood bank at a convenient time slot.

4. Track Impact

See your donation history and the lives you've helped from your dashboard.
