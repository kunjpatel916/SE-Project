Eligibility

Who can register as an organ donor?

  • Almost anyone, regardless of age, can register their intent to donate.
  • Medical suitability is assessed at the time of donation, not at registration.
  • Certain active infections or cancers may affect eligibility for specific organs.
  • You can register as both a blood and organ donor from a single account.
